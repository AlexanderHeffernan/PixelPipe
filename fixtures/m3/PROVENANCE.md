# M3 fixture provenance

The patch coordinates and replacement palette in this directory are synthetic
test data created specifically for PixelPipe. They contain no external artwork
and are available under `CC0-1.0` independently of the source-code license.

They operate on the synthetic M1 signal-flare raster and exercise explicit
transparent-index remapping, atomic coordinate edits, branching, comparison,
inspection, and review history. No Last Light, Painter, Shipyard, or third-party
image material is present.
