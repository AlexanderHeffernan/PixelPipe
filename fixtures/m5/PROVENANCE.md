# M5 fixture provenance

The fake one-shot agent and its generated 2×2 RGBA PNG are synthetic fixtures
created specifically for PixelPipe. They contain no external artwork and are
available under `CC0-1.0` independently of the source-code license.

The fake adapter uses only Python's standard library. It deterministically
exercises the public JSON protocol, candidate hashing, stderr capture/redaction,
critique, unapplied patch proposals, malformed responses, path traversal,
non-zero exits, and cancellation. It does not contact a model or provider. No
Last Light, Painter, Shipyard, PoC, or third-party image material is present.
